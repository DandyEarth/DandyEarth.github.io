Common questions

Can you help us meet IRS or FTC requirements?

Our security work supports your firm's compliance efforts; it does not, by itself, guarantee IRS, FTC, or other regulatory compliance. We put practical safeguards in place and document them so your compliance work is easier.

Can you be ready for tax season?

Yes. We keep workstations, email, and backups reliable through your busiest months, and respond quickly when something interrupts the work.

What happens if an email account is compromised?

We investigate, lock it down, recover the account, and tighten protections so it is far less likely to happen again.
